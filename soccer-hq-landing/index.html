What your free trial actually includes

Not a taster hour. Three full sessions over three weeks, so your child settles in properly and you can see the difference before deciding anything.

Week 1

Meet the Coaches

Your child completes a full session within their own age and ability group. Following the session, we’ll send you personalised feedback on their performance and development.

Week 2

Skills and technique

A ball at their feet for 90% of the session, developing close control, quick feet and confidence through fun, high-energy practices.

Week 3

Confidence and Creativity

Players put their new skills into practice through exciting challenges and small-group activities. This is where parents really start to notice their confidence grow.
